Aster Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026  ·  Contact: buildingaster@gmail.com

This Privacy Notice for Aster Health, Inc. ("Aster," "we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Visit our website at https://aster.fit or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Download and use our mobile application (Aster Period & Health Tracker), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Use Aster, an all-in-one health app that tracks periods and cycles, mood and hormonal changes, and food calories and nutrients, and that includes an optional AI chatbot to help you understand your body, mind, and habits in one place
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events

We take the privacy of your health information seriously. Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at buildingaster@gmail.com.

Our Core Privacy Commitments

Because Aster handles sensitive health and reproductive data, we hold ourselves to the following commitments:

  • We do not sell your personal information. Ever.
  • We do not use your health, cycle, reproductive, or fitness data for advertising, and we do not show third-party ads in the app.
  • We do not share your health data with third parties for advertising, data mining, or any use-based marketing purpose.
  • AI features that process your personal data are optional and require your consent. You can turn them off at any time.

Medical Disclaimer

IMPORTANT: The Aster app, including its AI-powered chatbot and all wellness insights, is intended for general informational and educational purposes only. Nothing in this app constitutes medical advice, medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or a substitute for professional medical advice from a licensed healthcare provider. The information provided by Aster — including cycle predictions, nutritional guidance, mood tracking summaries, and AI-generated insights — is educational wellness information only and should not be relied upon for medical decisions.

Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare professional with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition, medication, or health concern. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read or received through this app. If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services immediately.

Summary of Key Points

What personal information do we process? When you use our Services, we process the personal information you provide (such as account details and the health, cycle, mood, and nutrition data you choose to log) and limited technical information needed to run and improve the app.

Do we process sensitive personal information? Yes. Aster is a health app, so we process health and reproductive health data that you choose to enter. We process this sensitive information only to provide the features you request, and only with your consent or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Do we collect information from third parties? We do not collect personal information about you from third parties.

Do we sell your data or use it for advertising? No. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your health data for advertising or data mining.

How do we process your information? To provide, personalize, maintain, and improve the Services, to communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. Optional AI features process your data only with your consent.

With whom do we share personal information? Only with a small set of service providers that help us operate the app (such as analytics, AI processing, nutrition lookup, and subscription management), strictly as needed to provide the Services.

How do we keep your information safe? We use appropriate organizational and technical safeguards. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed 100% secure.

What are your rights? Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account and associated data directly in the app.

How do you exercise your rights? In the app (Settings > Account), by submitting a data subject access request at this link, or by contacting us at buildingaster@gmail.com.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect the personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register for the Services, use app features, contact us, or otherwise interact with us.

Personal Information Provided by You. Depending on how you use Aster, this may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username and password (account credentials), or a third-party sign-in (Sign in with Apple or Google)
  • Contact and communication preferences
  • The health, cycle, mood, and nutrition data you choose to log (see Sections 2 and 3)

Sensitive Information. Aster is a health app, so with your consent (or as otherwise permitted by law) we process the following categories of sensitive information that you choose to provide:

  • Health data, including reproductive and menstrual health data
  • Fitness and exercise data
  • Nutrition and dietary data

We process sensitive information only to provide the features you request. We do not process sensitive personal information for advertising, for sale, or to infer characteristics about you for marketing.

Payment and Subscription Data. If you purchase a subscription, the payment itself is processed by the Apple App Store (in-app purchase) and/or our subscription management provider, RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your full payment card number. We receive limited subscription status information (such as whether your subscription is active) to unlock paid features. RevenueCat's privacy notice is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Application Data. If you use our application, we may also collect the following if you choose to grant access or permission. You can change or revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device settings.

  • Camera and Photos: If you choose to, you can take a photo of a meal with your camera or select one from your photo library to attach to a food log. We access the camera and photo library only for this purpose and only when you initiate it.
  • Push Notifications: If you opt in, we may send push notifications about your account, cycle reminders, or app features. You can turn these off in your device settings.
  • Mobile Device Data: We automatically collect limited technical information such as device model, operating system and version, app version, device identifiers, and IP address, to operate the app, keep it secure, and diagnose problems.

Information automatically collected

In Short: Some limited technical information is collected automatically to run, secure, and improve the Services.

When you use the Services we automatically collect service-related, diagnostic, usage, and performance information, such as your IP address, device characteristics, operating system, app version, and information about how you interact with the app (for example, which screens or features you use and crash or error reports). This information is used to operate, secure, and improve the Services, and is not used for advertising.

Google API

If you sign in with Google, our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. App-Specific Data We Collect

In Short: This section describes, specifically and transparently, the categories of data collected through your direct use of Aster's core features.

Food Logs and Meal Entries

  • Food items you manually log or add via barcode scan
  • Meal timestamps and portion sizes
  • Calorie and nutrient data associated with logged foods
  • Meal photos you choose to take or attach to a food log
  • Barcode data used to retrieve nutritional information from Open Food Facts (an open-source nutrition database)

Cycle Tracking Entries

  • Period start and end dates
  • Symptoms you choose to log (e.g., cramps, headaches, energy levels, mood)
  • Wellness and lifestyle logs associated with your cycle (e.g., sleep, stress, exercise, water intake)
  • Notes and observations you voluntarily enter

Optional Chatbot Messages

  • Messages you send to Aster's AI chatbot (typed or spoken)
  • The chatbot's responses, which are generated using third-party AI services
  • Contextual data from your profile (such as cycle stage or recent logs) used to personalize responses, only when you have enabled AI processing

Device and App Analytics

  • Feature usage data (which screens and features you interact with)
  • App events (e.g., button taps, navigation flows, feature activations)
  • App and device diagnostics (e.g., crash reports, error logs, performance metrics)
  • Session duration and frequency data
  • Session replays (screen recordings used to understand and improve app flows). Text you type and images are automatically masked, and replays are only captured when you have enabled analytics.

This data is collected only to operate, maintain, and improve the features of the Aster app. You control whether your data is used for AI processing and analytics through the Settings menu, as described in Section 9.

3. Health Data

In Short: We treat your health data with special care. We never use it for advertising, never sell it, and only share it as strictly necessary to provide features you have requested.

Aster processes health-related data that you voluntarily enter (such as cycle, symptom, mood, and nutrition data) to provide the app's core features — including period predictions, nutritional summaries, symptom and mood pattern visualizations, and your personal wellness dashboard.

Your health data is processed on our secure servers and is used only to provide the features you request. It is not sold, not used for advertising or data mining, and not shared with third parties except the limited service providers described in Section 8 (for example, optional AI processing that you have turned on) — and never for advertising.

Aster does not currently integrate with Apple Health (HealthKit) or Google Fit. If we add such an integration in the future, we will update this Privacy Notice first, and any connection will be optional and will require your permission.

5. How Do We Use Your Data in the App?

In Short: The data you enter into Aster is used only to provide the features you request, generate optional AI-powered insights (with your consent), keep the app reliable, and measure feature usage to improve the app.

Your cycle tracking entries, food logs, and wellness logs are used to power Aster's core features, including period predictions, nutritional summaries, mood and symptom pattern visualizations, and your personal wellness dashboard. 8. Third-Party Data Processing

In Short: Aster uses a limited set of trusted service providers to power specific features. Data is shared only as strictly necessary, and never for advertising.

PostHog (Product Analytics)

We share app usage and device analytics data (such as feature usage events, session data, and crash diagnostics) with PostHog to understand how the app is used, identify issues, and improve performance. PostHog processes this data on our behalf and does not use it for its own advertising purposes. Privacy information: posthog.com/privacy

AI Processing Services (Optional Wellness Insights and Chatbot)

When you have enabled AI processing (see Section 9), limited personal data — such as food entries, cycle data, symptoms, meal photos, or chatbot messages — is transmitted through secure, server-side processing to third-party AI providers to generate your personalized wellness insights and chatbot responses. These providers include Google Cloud AI (Vertex/Gemini), Anthropic, OpenAI, Amazon Web Services (AWS) AI, and Speechify (for optional text-to-speech). Under our agreements and these providers' API terms, your data is used solely to generate responses to you; it is not used to train their models and is not used for advertising. If you do not enable AI processing, no data is sent to these providers. You can disable AI processing at any time in Settings.

Open Food Facts (Nutrition Lookup)

When you use the barcode scanner, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free and open-source food product database, to retrieve nutritional information. Only the barcode value is transmitted; no personal identifying information is sent to Open Food Facts. Privacy information: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

RevenueCat and the App Store (Subscriptions)

Subscription purchases are processed by the Apple App Store and managed by RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your full payment card number. We receive limited subscription status information to unlock paid features. Privacy information: revenuecat.com/privacy

We share data with each provider above only as needed to provide the specific feature described, and never for advertising or any additional commercial purpose.

9. User Controls and Consent

In Short: You are in control of how your data is used for AI insights and analytics. You can enable or disable these at any time in Settings.

AI Processing and Analytics Controls

You can enable or disable AI processing and analytics data sharing directly within the app at any time: Settings > Data & AI Processing

  • When ENABLED: Limited personal data (such as food entries, cycle data, and chatbot messages) may be sent to our AI processing providers to generate personalized wellness insights and chatbot responses, and app usage data is shared with PostHog for analytics.
  • When DISABLED: AI processing of your personal data is turned off and no data is sent to AI providers, and analytics sharing with PostHog is turned off. You may continue to use Aster's core tracking features; AI-powered insights and chatbot personalization will be unavailable while this setting is off.

AI features and the processing of your sensitive health data for AI insights are optional and based on your consent. We ask for your consent before using your personal data for AI features, and you can change your choice at any time without penalty. Disabling AI processing does not affect your access to Aster's core cycle tracking and nutrition logging features.

Withdrawing Consent

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by updating Settings > Data & AI Processing in the app, contacting us at buildingaster@gmail.com, or submitting a data subject access request. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.

Opting Out of Marketing Communications

We only send marketing, reminder, or promotional messages by push notification or email where you have opted in. You can opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe link in an email, turning off notifications in your device settings, or contacting us at buildingaster@gmail.com. You may still receive non-marketing service messages necessary to administer your account.
